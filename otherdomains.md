Top 5 Domain Applications (Beyond Education Loans)
1. HR & Talent Acquisition (ATS – Applicant Tracking Systems)
Problem: AI-powered resume screening & hiring decisions silently discriminate by gender, age, location, or educational background.

GenAI + Human-in-the-Loop Solution:

GenAI generates diverse synthetic resumes (matching/non-matching qualified candidates across demographics)

System scores candidates with fair vs. biased ATS models

Metrics: Screening parity by gender/age, interview call-back disparities

Humans validate unfair patterns, GenAI refines scoring logic

Impact: Deloitte could sell this to Fortune 500 HR teams as a pre-deployment fairness audit tool

Why it's strong: High regulatory risk (EEOC compliance), immediate market need, clear business value

2. Credit Risk & Financial Services (Lending, Credit Cards, Insurance)
Problem: Banks/credit agencies discriminate in credit decisions, interest rates, or insurance premiums based on protected attributes.

GenAI + Human-in-the-Loop Solution:

GenAI generates synthetic applicant profiles with varied income, location, credit history, family status

Dual-mode scoring (fair vs. biased) shows approval & rate disparities

Metrics: Approval parity, interest rate gaps, insurance premium bias

Humans flag unfair patterns, GenAI proposes mitigation (e.g., "cap rate differences at 0.5%")

Impact: Directly applicable to mortgages, auto loans, credit cards, insurance underwriting

Why it's strong: Massive market (every bank needs this), regulatory compliance (Fair Lending Act), your education loan project is a subset of this

3. Healthcare & Medical Diagnosis AI
Problem: Clinical AI models trained on skewed datasets may diagnose/treat certain demographics unfairly (e.g., fewer chest pain alerts for women, lower risk scores for minorities).

GenAI + Human-in-the-Loop Solution:

GenAI creates synthetic patient profiles (demographics, symptoms, test results) covering diverse populations

AI diagnostic model scores patients with fair vs. biased logic

Metrics: Diagnosis disparity by race/gender, treatment recommendation parity, false-negative rates by demographic

Doctors/clinicians review findings and suggest fairness improvements

GenAI refines diagnostic prompts/thresholds and re-tests

Impact: Healthcare institutions, medical device makers, telemedicine platforms need bias audits

Why it's strong: Life-critical decisions, strong regulatory pressure (FDA, healthcare compliance), growing market demand

4. Criminal Justice & Predictive Policing (Risk Assessment)
Problem: Risk assessment tools (COMPAS, etc.) used in bail/sentencing decisions systematically discriminate by race or socioeconomic status.

GenAI + Human-in-the-Loop Solution:

GenAI generates synthetic criminal history profiles (crime type, prior convictions, demographics, neighborhood)

System scores risk with fair vs. biased logic

Metrics: Risk score parity by race, disparate impact in bail recommendations, sentencing bias

Judges/legal experts validate and suggest fairness constraints

GenAI refines risk logic and re-runs tests

Impact: Courts, prosecutors, bail systems, criminal justice agencies

Why it's strong: Highest stakes (liberty/justice), strong advocacy pressure, regulatory scrutiny, growing awareness of algorithmic bias

5. E-Commerce & Recommendation Systems (Pricing, Ads, Targeting)
Problem: AI pricing engines, product recommendations, or ad targeting algorithms discriminate by user demographics (location, browsing history, economic status).

GenAI + Human-in-the-Loop Solution:

GenAI creates synthetic user profiles (demographics, purchase history, location, income proxy)

Dual-mode scoring shows if pricing/recommendations are fair across groups

Metrics: Price disparity (same product different prices?), recommendation diversity, ad impression parity

Product/marketing teams validate and flag unfair patterns

GenAI adjusts recommendation logic and re-tests

Impact: Marketplaces (Amazon, eBay), social media platforms (Meta, TikTok), travel sites (Airbnb, booking.com)

Why it's strong: Massive scale, consumer trust issue, regulatory pressure (EU algorithms directive), competitive differentiator
