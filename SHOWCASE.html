Enterprise-grade validation platform for detecting and mitigating bias in AI loan approval systems

Project Overview

This MVP provides a comprehensive validation platform for detecting and mitigating bias in education loan approval AI systems. The platform uses Generative AI to generate realistic student profiles, score them with both fair and biased models, detect bias patterns across 5 dimensions, validate findings with human domain experts, and mitigate bias through iterative GenAI prompt improvements.

Key Features

🎯

Synthetic Profile Generation

GenAI generates realistic Indian student loan profiles with geographic diversity, income distribution, CIBIL scores, and educational backgrounds. Includes edge cases like first-time borrowers and self-employed applicants.

⚖️

Dual-Mode Scoring

Fair Scoring: Evaluates based on academic merit, creditworthiness, and repayment capacity only. Biased Scoring: Simulates unconscious bias (rural penalty, income discrimination, etc.)

🔍

5-Dimensional Bias Detection

Comprehensive bias analysis across Geographic (Urban vs. Rural), Income (High vs. Low), Gender & Co-applicant, Credit Score Logic, and Robustness & Edge Cases.

📊

Quantified Bias Metrics

Five key metrics: Approval Rate Parity (≥0.95), Interest Rate Disparity (<0.5%), Collateral Requirement Gap (<10%), Edge Case Coverage (≥95%), and Overall Fairness Score (≥85/100).

👥

Human-in-the-Loop Validation

Dashboard with KPI cards, heatmap, and top findings. Detailed bias analysis with filterable table. Human feedback form for annotations and root cause analysis.

🔄

Agentic Mitigation Loop

GenAI reads human feedback and refines prompts iteratively. Re-scores all profiles with improved prompts, recalculates metrics, and validates improvement until target fairness is achieved.
